## Known caveats

- `REPORT_ASOS.md` — the three-station section is the original subset; the
  eight-station ladder is below it. The "close to zero" wording was corrected
  to the measured range.
- `REPORT_CLOSURE.md` — generated by `run_closure.py`. The 0.907 value is
  `corr(T_true(k), T_surr(k))` across wavenumbers, not a shell sum. The surrogate
  fails solenoidality; its transfer shape is correlated with truth.
- `REPORT_THEORY.md` — Section 7's T(k)-uncorrelated sub-prediction is marked as
  not supported by the measured 0.907 shape correlation.
- `REPORT_NONMARKOV_ARGO.md` — 3/4 pre-specified; the 28 Argo float IDs are
  not archived.
- `REPORT_REANALYSIS.md` — the 4.0%/4.5% values are `KE_div/KE_rot` ratios,
  not shares of total kinetic energy.
